QUICK ANSWERS

Before you write.

These answers may help you get back to enjoying the app.

Automatic mode isn’t responding?

On the Home screen, open “Not working? Check it”. The guide lets you check the microphone, bark detection, and sound playback without using daily detections.

Make sure Silence Dog has microphone permission and review sensitivity in the app’s settings. If you use the free version, also check your available detections.

I can’t hear the sound. What should I check?

Try the sound with “Calm now”, check your device volume, and see whether audio is being sent to headphones or a Bluetooth speaker. The check guide also lets you test playback.

I already purchased Premium. How do I restore it?

Open Settings in Silence Dog and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple Account you used for the purchase. App updates preserve access to valid purchases.

If access isn’t restored, email us and describe what happens. We don’t need your password or card details.

How does Find your sound work?

Choose a situation, try a sound, and record the reaction you observe in your dog. Save notes and favorites, and compare observations from similar situations. The guided experience is available to everyone; usable sounds depend on your current access.

Ratings reflect your observations and do not establish that a sound causes calm.

Can I suggest an improvement?

Absolutely. Tell us what you would like to do and when it would be useful. Your feedback helps shape the next improvements to Silence Dog.
